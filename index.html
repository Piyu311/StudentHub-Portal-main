Our Clubs

Technical Club

The Technical Club focuses on electronics, hardware projects and innovation. Students work on circuits, Arduino, and IoT devices together.

Coding Club

The Coding Club is for students who love programming. We hold hackathons, coding contests and help members learn new languages and frameworks.

Robotics Club

The Robotics Club builds and programs robots. Members participate in national competitions and explore areas like AI and drone technology.
